# 📊 QUETICO - Executive Financial Analysis & Dashboard

A comprehensive Power BI financial dashboard analyzing the financial performance, profitability, liquidity, and solvency of **QUETICO** from 2021 to 2025.

## 🚀 Key Highlights & Insights

* **Executive Dashboard:** Tracks core financial metrics including Total Revenue (67.59K), Net Income (8.88K), Net Margin (13.14%), Current Ratio (1.44), and Debt-to-Equity Ratio (3.62)[cite: 1].
* **Revenue vs. Profitability:** While revenue continues to grow steadily, net income declined in 2025 (by -17.7%), indicating growing pressure on profit margins[cite: 1].
* **Margin Compression & Cost Pressures:** A widening gap between revenue and gross profit highlights that Cost of Goods Sold (COGS) is rising faster than revenue[cite: 1]. Furthermore, increasing non-operating charges and financing costs (driven by high financial leverage) further erode profit before reaching the bottom line[cite: 1].
* **Liquidity & Solvency:** Working capital remained positive throughout the analysis period and increased significantly in 2025 (reaching 15.93K), showcasing strong short-term financial flexibility[cite: 2]. The current ratio consistently stayed above 1.0, ensuring healthy liquidity despite high financial risk/leverage[cite: 1, 2].

## 🛠️ Tools & Technologies
* **Power BI:** Data modeling, DAX measures, and interactive data visualization.
* **Financial Metrics Analyzed:** ROI, Net Margin, Operating Margins, Debt-to-Equity, Current Ratio, and Working Capital Trends.
